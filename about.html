Our Story

Crafting Freshness Since 2018

DRYME started with a simple idea: everyone deserves premium garment care without the hassle. What began as a small neighborhood dry cleaner has grown into a trusted service trusted by over 15,000 customers across the city.

We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern, eco-friendly technology. Every garment that passes through our hands gets the attention it deserves — because we know your clothes are more than just fabric.

Our Process

Where Science Meets Craftsmanship

Every garment goes through a meticulous 7-step process: inspection, pre-treatment, specialized cleaning, quality check, pressing, finishing, and final review.

Our facility uses advanced hydrocarbon cleaning technology that's gentle on fabrics but tough on stains. Combined with hand-finishing by experienced professionals, the result is garments that look and feel their absolute best.

We handle over 200 fabric types and have specialized processes for wedding dresses, leather, suede, and evening wear.
